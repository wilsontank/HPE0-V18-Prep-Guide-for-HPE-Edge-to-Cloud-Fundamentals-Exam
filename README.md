## HPE0-V18 Exam Objectives

The exam blueprint is strongly focused on identifying customer requirements and positioning appropriate HPE edge-to-cloud solutions.

### 1. Gather and Analyze Customer Business and Technical Requirements — 5%

This domain focuses on identifying what the customer is trying to achieve, including business goals, workload needs, infrastructure requirements, constraints, and technical priorities. Candidates should be able to distinguish business requirements from technical requirements and use both to guide solution decisions.

### 2. Recommend and Position HPE Edge-to-Cloud Offerings — 25%

This is the largest individual domain. It tests your ability to identify the most suitable HPE solutions, products, and services for different customer use cases. You should understand how HPE offerings address common requirements such as modernization, scalability, cloud operating models, security, and workload placement.

### 3. Review an HPE Edge-to-Cloud Solution Based on Customer Needs — 10%

Candidates must be able to review a proposed solution and determine whether it aligns with the customer’s requirements. This includes checking whether the solution addresses business goals, technical constraints, operational expectations, and future growth.

### 4. Position HPE Edge-to-Cloud Solutions and Alliance Partner Components — 20%

This domain tests your ability to combine HPE solutions with appropriate workload technologies and alliance partner components. You should understand how different ecosystem technologies can support a complete customer solution rather than treating HPE products in isolation.

### 5. Prepare an HPE Edge-to-Cloud Proposal — 10%

This section focuses on turning customer requirements into a practical proposal. Candidates should understand how to expand an existing environment, introduce new solutions, and explain how a proposed architecture supports current and future business needs.

### 6. Describe HPE Edge-to-Cloud Solutions Management — 5%

This domain covers the management layer used to operate and oversee edge-to-cloud environments. Focus on how centralized management, visibility, lifecycle operations, and cloud-like management experiences support customers across distributed infrastructure.

### 7. Explain HPE Private Cloud Solutions — 15%

Candidates should understand the role of HPE private cloud solutions and how they support organizations that want cloud-like flexibility while maintaining control over infrastructure and data. Pay attention to use cases involving hybrid cloud, workload placement, operational consistency, and private cloud consumption models.

### 8. Describe HPE Services — 5%

This section covers the value of HPE services and how they support customers throughout the solution lifecycle. Important areas include the HPE Services value proposition, HPE Juniper Networking support services, and services associated with private cloud solutions.

### 9. Display an Ability to Build an HPE Edge-to-Cloud Solution — 5%

This domain brings the other objectives together. Candidates may need to evaluate requirements, select appropriate technologies, and assemble a solution that aligns with customer business and technical needs.

- When does private cloud make sense?
- Which partner technology should be included?
- How should an existing environment be expanded?
- What business value does the proposed solution provide?
